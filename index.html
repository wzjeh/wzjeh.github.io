Research

I work on data-driven flow chemistry: getting structured experimental data out of the literature, generating it with automated flow platforms, and using it efficiently for optimization.

  • Multimodal mining of structured data from chemistry literature
    Computer vision, OCR, structured data extraction
  • Low-cost automated flow chemistry platform
    Lab automation, open hardware, in-line analysis, Bayesian optimization
  • Heterogeneous catalysts for continuous flow
    Microreactors, heterogeneous catalysis, process development
